The Bovine News Network is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of ability or technology.

## Our Standard

We aim to meet **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** across the site, including semantic markup, keyboard operability, sufficient color contrast, and support for reduced motion preferences.

## Ongoing Work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review the site using automated tools and manual testing, including screen readers and keyboard-only navigation.

## Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, please let us know through our [contact page](/contact/) so we can address it.
