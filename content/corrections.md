The Bovine News Network is committed to accuracy. When we get something wrong, we say so here, in public, in this exact font. Corrections newer than seven days also run on air, immediately after the weather and before whatever Gary has prepared.

---

**Aug. 22, 2026 —** A story about the barn dance stated the headliner was "to be announced." The headliner remains to be announced. However, we have since learned that this was also true at press time, which makes our original story accurate and this correction ceremonial. We regret your reading time.

**Aug. 19, 2026 —** An article about dairy prices described the herd as "taking it personally." Several members of the herd have written in to clarify that they are taking it *professionally*. The story has been updated and two friendships have not recovered.

**Aug. 17, 2026 —** In our coverage of the intersection meeting, we reported that the meeting "could have been an email." We have since confirmed that no member of the herd uses email. The meeting could not, in any practical sense, have been an email. We regret the error and the six hours.

**Aug. 14, 2026 —** A previous version of this site's trending bar listed "Gary the Banjo Guy" without his full title. His full title is Gary the Banjo Guy, Esq. Bovine News apologizes for the omission and for assuming.

**Aug. 13, 2026 —** Early coverage of the north pasture incident stated that hoofprints led "to and from" the salad bar. Forensic review confirms the prints lead *toward* the salad bar, pause, and then return, a gait pattern experts describe as "reconsidering." The distinction matters and we apologize for flattening it.

**Aug. 13, 2026 —** This desk initially reported the number of ranch dressing packets as "a wheelbarrow's worth." The verified count is thirty-seven. We have updated the figure and would like to state, for the record, that counting them twice was not fun.

**Jul. 30, 2026 —** A weather segment forecast "partly cloudy" conditions for the western pasture. Conditions were, by every measurement available to this newsroom, fully cloudy. Meteorologist Mooira Nav stands by her forecast, noting that the clouds were only partly *trying*.

**Jun. 2, 2025 —** In February we credited "the wind" with opening the north pasture gate. We now understand the wind was innocent, the gate was operated politely, and the county knew both things when it briefed us. A joint investigation with ourselves is underway.
