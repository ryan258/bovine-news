  - heading: "Our Mission"
    body: "The Bovine News Network exists to deliver accurate, timely, and thorough coverage of the community we serve. We believe every resident — human or otherwise — deserves a newsroom they can trust."
  - heading: "Editorial Standards"
    body: "Every story published by Bovine News is reviewed for accuracy, sourcing, and fairness before it reaches our audience. We correct errors publicly and promptly, and we do not publish unverified claims as fact."
  - heading: "Newsroom History"
    body: "Bovine News began as a single-camera community bulletin covering county fair results and grazing permits. Over the years it has grown into a full broadcast operation with a dedicated investigations desk, a weather center, and a sports team of its own."
  - heading: "Broadcast Philosophy"
    body: "We treat every story with the same seriousness, whether it's a city council vote or a gate left open overnight. The news doesn't get to decide what's worth taking seriously — we do, and we take all of it seriously."
  - heading: "Community Commitment"
    body: "Bovine News is committed to covering the stories that matter to this community, not just the ones that are easy to tell. We show up to the hearings, the fairs, and the pastures, because that's where the news actually happens."
