Regional shipments of ranch dressing through the Water Trough Strait — the narrow corridor between the community water trough and the east fence — remained stalled for a tenth consecutive week Tuesday, as the vessel blocking the channel continued to go unacknowledged by every party with the authority to move it.

## A Chokepoint Under Strain

The obstruction is a wheelbarrow containing thirty-seven empty ranch dressing packets, positioned athwart the corridor since mid-June. County code enforcement has declined to relocate it, citing jurisdictional ambiguity. The salad bar, twelve feet downwind, has declined to claim it, citing nothing, verbally, while maintaining what maritime observers describe as "aggressive eye contact with the horizon."

Traffic through the strait has dropped to a single determined duck. Delivery schedules published this week show dressing resupply pushed to late September, with a spokesperson for the distributor noting that alternate routes exist but "cross somebody's pasture, and given recent events, we are not doing that."

## The Strategic Reserve

In response, the salad bar has formally reclassified the thirty-seven packets as a strategic reserve, a designation that changes nothing physically but allows management to discuss them in the plural tense during emergencies.

"We have options," said a salad bar representative, reading from a card. "We have a reserve. We are comfortable. When pressed on whether comfort and supply are the same thing, the representative ate the card."

Investigators reviewing the wheelbarrow's chain of custody for an ongoing inquiry tell this desk that the packet count has now been revised upward twice and confirmed twice, and that whoever left the wheelbarrow "parked it better than most people park cars."

## What Happens Next

The county has proposed a voluntary convoy system using the west gate, which would require opening the west gate. As of press time, the west gate has been found open anyway, unlatched neatly against the fence, with no note.

Residents wishing to conserve dressing are advised to consider vinaigrette, a suggestion that has been described by one longtime resident as "the kind of talk that gets a county restructured."
