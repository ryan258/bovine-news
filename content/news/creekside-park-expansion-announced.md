Following last week's council approval, city officials confirmed Tuesday that construction on the Creekside Park expansion will officially break ground this fall, with the first phase targeted for completion before next summer's festival season and the long-contested bench confirmed, for the third and hopefully final time, to be staying exactly where it is.

## What's Coming

The approved expansion includes a new walking trail along the creek's western bank, a covered pavilion for community events, and an expanded grazing lawn, plus what officials are calling "a modest, tasteful, extremely litigated bench."

"We heard from a lot of residents during the hearing process, and I think this plan reflects that input, all four hundred hoofprints of it," said a spokesperson for the parks department, holding up the calf-play-area petition for the cameras.

## Phased Construction

Officials outlined a two-phase build to minimize disruption:

- Phase one covers the walking trail and pavilion, targeted for spring completion.
- Phase two covers the grazing lawn expansion, expected to wrap by early summer.
- The existing playground area will remain open throughout construction, with the exception of one afternoon reserved for what the schedule vaguely calls "bench installation, ceremonial."
- A commemorative plaque will be installed at the bench, at the request of exactly one resident, reading only: "IT FACES NORTH NOW. WE'RE FINE."

## Community Reaction

Local businesses near the park say they expect the improvements to bring more foot traffic during festival season, though several have raised concerns about parking during construction, prompting the parks department to remind everyone, gently, that the parking lot was the whole reason this took a year.
