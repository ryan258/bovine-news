The Bovine Weather Center has issued a severe thunderstorm watch for the western pasture through 9 p.m. tonight, with meteorologists warning of gusty winds and heavy rainfall, and chief meteorologist Mooira Nav warning, separately, that she has been waiting for this specific front since Tuesday and has already laminated three maps.

## What to Expect

Conditions are expected to deteriorate through the afternoon as a cold front moves through the region, a sentence Mooira has now delivered to camera four times with four distinct and increasingly theatrical hand gestures.

- Wind gusts up to 45 mph are possible during the strongest cells, which Mooira describes as "not dangerous, exactly, but extremely exciting, and I will be standing outside for it."
- Rainfall totals could exceed an inch and a half in the heaviest areas, a figure the weather desk has already printed onto a small commemorative chart.
- Isolated hail is possible but not the primary concern, though studio staff report Mooira has, unofficially, been rooting for it since sunrise.

"Residents are advised to bring in loose hay and secure any lightweight equipment before the front arrives," said chief meteorologist Mooira Nav, who then held up a felt cutout of a cloud she made specifically for tonight's broadcast.

## Safety Reminders

The weather desk recommends staying indoors during the height of the storm and avoiding low-lying areas prone to flash flooding along the creek. Mooira, for her part, will not be doing either of those things and has already informed her editor.
