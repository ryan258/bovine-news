Authorities are asking residents to avoid the north pasture while investigators determine who, or conceivably what, opened the main gate sometime between 11 p.m. and 4 a.m., according to a statement released this morning by the county grazing authority and read aloud four times because nobody in the room could believe it.

## What Happened

A routine perimeter check discovered the north pasture's main gate standing fully open shortly before dawn, latch undamaged, hinges freshly oiled by a hand — or hoof — investigators describe only as "disturbingly competent." No livestock were reported missing at the time of publication, though a full headcount is underway and complicated by the fact that at least six cows have alibis that place them "definitely inside, definitely asleep, why do you ask."

"We are treating this as a serious breach of pasture security until we have evidence to the contrary," said a spokesperson for the grazing authority, declining to speculate on motive while standing directly beside a wheelbarrow of empty ranch-dressing packets that nobody would acknowledge.

## Investigation Underway

Investigative reporter Bessie Barnes has reviewed preliminary incident logs and confirmed the following details:

- The gate's latch mechanism showed no signs of forced entry, but did show what forensic technicians are calling "suspiciously good manners."
- Security footage from a nearby feed shed was reportedly inconclusive, consisting mainly of a single cow staring directly into the lens for eleven unbroken minutes.
- A nearly identical incident was recorded at the same gate eighteen months ago and was never resolved, though at the time it was blamed on "the wind," a theory investigators now admit was "generous."
- A trail of hoofprints leads from the gate to the community salad bar and then, mysteriously, back again, as if whoever did it got there, reconsidered, and left a very polite note.

> "The herd deserves to know," said Barnes, who has slept at her desk for two consecutive nights and would like everyone to know that this is a normal and sustainable way to run an investigation.

## What Happens Next

The county has asked residents to avoid the immediate area while technicians repair the latch and investigators complete their canvass, a process complicated by the fact that several potential witnesses keep chewing thoughtfully and refusing to make eye contact. Bovine News will update this story as new information becomes available, or as someone finally cracks.
