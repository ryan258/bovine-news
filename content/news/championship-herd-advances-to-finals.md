The Bentonville Bulls clinched their division Saturday night with a 27-24 overtime win over the Dairy County Herd, setting up a championship showdown against Pasture United later this month, and igniting an instant, stadium-wide dispute over whether the winning play was a legal charge or, as the losing side maintains, "just a cow running very fast in a straight line."

## The Game

The Bulls trailed by ten points entering the fourth quarter before a late rally forced overtime, capped by a game-winning drive that took less than ninety seconds off the clock and ended with three officials, a booth review, and a scoreboard operator who admitted afterward to "just guessing" on the final ruling.

"We knew we had it in us, we just needed to settle in," said the Bulls' captain following the win, chewing with the slow, deliberate confidence of a cow who has not yet watched the replay and does not intend to.

## By the Numbers

- The Bulls finish the regular season 11-2-1, their best record in franchise history, a fact the team's mascot has already had embroidered onto four separate items.
- Saturday's win marks their fourth consecutive overtime victory this season, a streak the opposing coach called "statistically suspicious" and "personally exhausting."
- Pasture United enters the championship match with a 9-4-1 record and, per their coach, "a very long memory."

## Looking Ahead

The championship match is scheduled for later this month. Full coverage, including ticket information, pre-game analysis, and a promised second opinion on the charge from a retired referee who has already texted this reporter six times unprompted, will be available on the Bovine News sports desk.
