Organizers announced Thursday the headlining act for this year's Barn Dance Festival — a traveling human banjo player known only as "Gary" — capping months of speculation among festival regulars and opening an entirely new debate over whether the festival has, as one longtime attendee put it, "lost the thread."

## The Announcement

The lineup reveal was made during a brief ceremony at the festival grounds, where organizers also confirmed expanded food vendor space, a new second stage, and, in response to nobody's question, that Gary is "very good, actually, just come see."

"This is our biggest lineup yet, and we wanted to make sure everyone had room to actually enjoy it," said a festival spokesperson, gently sidestepping a follow-up question about the banjo situation for the third time.

## What to Expect

- The festival will run three days this year, up from two, giving skeptics ample time to come around.
- A new family-friendly stage will host acts earlier in the evening, all of them cows, organizers confirmed, unprompted and a little defensively.
- Advance tickets go on sale next week through the festival's website, alongside a newly added FAQ entry titled "Is The Headliner a Person? Yes. We Know."

## Community Response

Local businesses along the festival route say they are preparing for record turnout, with several already extending hours for the weekend, while a small but organized group calling itself Cows for Cow Headliners has begun circulating a petition demanding "at minimum, a duet."
