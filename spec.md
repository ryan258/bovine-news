# Bovine News Network Website
## Waterfall Project Description & Development Specification

**Project Type:** Satirical local-news website / fictional broadcast brand  
**Working Title:** Bovine News Network  
**Primary Brand Line:** **No Bull. Just Moo-Point.**  
**Primary Deliverable:** Fully responsive public-facing website based on the approved Bovine News Team visual mockup  
**Development Methodology:** Traditional Waterfall  
**Primary Experience:** Desktop-first responsive newsroom website with strong mobile support  
**Content Style:** Professional local television news presentation combined with intentionally absurd bovine journalism

---

# 1. Project Overview

The Bovine News Network website is a complete fictional digital news property built around a team of plush cow journalists operating as a serious television newsroom.

The central creative joke is that every visual and functional convention of a professional local news organization is treated seriously, while the reporters, anchors, meteorologists, producers, sports personalities, and field correspondents are stuffed cows.

The site should never feel like a loose collection of cow jokes.

It should feel like a **real, fully funded regional television news operation that happens to be staffed entirely by cows**.

The quality of the design, typography, information hierarchy, navigation, breaking-news presentation, weather graphics, sports coverage, and broadcast imagery should be strong enough that a visitor initially reads the interface as a legitimate modern local-news website.

The humor emerges from the contrast.

The website will provide:

- Breaking news
- Local stories
- Community coverage
- Weather
- Sports
- Feature stories
- Reporter profiles
- Video and livestream presentation
- Search
- Newsletter signup
- Social media links
- Article pages
- Category landing pages
- Multimedia content
- Promotional newsroom content

The initial homepage mockup establishes the visual direction for the complete site.

---

# 2. Project Vision

The finished Bovine News Network should visually occupy the space between:

- a modern regional television news station,
- a national cable-news digital property,
- a polished community news portal,
- and a fictional comedy universe.

The experience should be credible enough to support continuing stories, characters, fictional reporting, recurring jokes, video clips, social content, fake investigations, sports coverage, weather events, and larger narrative campaigns.

The site should therefore be designed not only as a single novelty homepage but as a **scalable editorial publishing system**.

The project should support expansion into a broader Bovine News universe.

# 4. Development Methodology

The website will follow a **Waterfall development model**.

Each major phase must be completed and approved before the next phase begins.

The development sequence will be:

1. Discovery
2. Requirements Definition
3. Information Architecture
4. Content Architecture
5. Visual Design
6. Technical Architecture
7. Front-End Development
8. Back-End / CMS Development
9. Content Production
10. Integration
11. Quality Assurance
12. Accessibility Review
13. Performance Optimization
14. User Acceptance Testing
15. Deployment
16. Post-Launch Validation
17. Maintenance

Changes introduced after a phase is approved should be treated as formal change requests.

This protects the project from uncontrolled scope expansion.

# 5. Phase 1 — Discovery

## Objective

Define exactly what the Bovine News Network is, who it serves, what content it publishes, how broad the fictional universe will become, and what technical requirements the finished product must satisfy.

## Discovery Questions

The team must determine:

- Is Bovine News primarily a parody?
- Is it an ongoing fictional newsroom?
- Will real news ever be parodied?
- Will stories be entirely fictional?
- Will the site contain video?
- Will the site eventually publish AI-generated broadcast clips?
- Will stories have actual timestamps and dates?
- Will users be able to subscribe?
- Will visitors be able to search archives?
- Will stories be shareable?
- Will individual reporters have author pages?
- Will the newsroom cover a fictional city or a real location?
- Will there be recurring fictional businesses, sports teams, or institutions?
- Will weather use real data, fictional data, or both?
- Will live-stream content eventually exist?
- Will stories be manually created or generated through a publishing workflow?

## Discovery Deliverables

The discovery phase produces:

- Project charter
- Brand positioning statement
- Audience definition
- Competitive inspiration list
- Functional feature list
- Preliminary sitemap
- Technical constraints
- Content ownership rules
- Tone guidelines
- Initial character roster
- Project risks
- Preliminary launch scope

## Approval Gate

No architecture or design work should begin until the project charter is approved.

# 7. Business Requirements

The website must:

- establish the Bovine News Network as a recognizable fictional media brand;
- allow continued expansion without redesigning the entire platform;
- support frequent publication;
- create reusable visual assets for social media and video;
- provide a strong home for recurring characters;
- support promotional campaigns;
- allow visitors to discover older stories;
- provide opportunities for newsletter or audience growth;
- preserve consistent brand presentation.

# 8. User Requirements

Visitors must be able to:

- understand the purpose of the site immediately;
- identify current top stories;
- access breaking news quickly;
- browse categories;
- read individual stories;
- identify the reporter responsible for a story;
- navigate to reporter profiles;
- view weather information;
- access sports content;
- watch video;
- access livestream content when available;
- search stories;
- subscribe to updates;
- navigate effectively on phone, tablet, and desktop;
- use the site with keyboard navigation;
- use the site with screen-reader technology;
- reduce motion when preferred.

# 57. Editorial Tone Guide

Bovine News should maintain a restrained comedic voice.

The journalists should behave as though the stories are completely legitimate.

Avoid constant cow puns.

Humor works best when the newsroom treats absurd events seriously.

Good:

> Authorities are asking residents to avoid the north pasture while investigators determine who opened the gate.

Less effective:

> This udderly moo-velous moo-situation has everyone mooing!

The site should choose the first approach.

- keyboard-only navigation,
- screen-reader testing,
- color contrast review,
- zoom to 200%,
- reduced-motion testing,
- form-error review,
- heading structure analysis.

Recommended tools:

- axe DevTools
- Lighthouse
- WAVE
- VoiceOver
- NVDA

---

# 62. Performance QA

Measure:

- image size,
- unused JavaScript,
- unused CSS,
- font loading,
- caching,
- layout shift,
- hero media performance.

---

# 63. User Acceptance Testing

UAT should answer:

- Can users understand the site immediately?
- Does the site read as professional news first?
- Is the humor clear without overpowering usability?
- Can visitors find stories quickly?
- Does mobile feel intentional?
- Are characters distinguishable?
- Does the site feel like one coherent media brand?

UAT approval is required before launch.

# 73. Final Product Identity

The finished Bovine News website should feel like a real newsroom brand rather than a single joke.

A visitor should be able to imagine:

- watching the evening broadcast,
- following individual reporters,
- checking the weather every morning,
- reading local stories,
- watching sports highlights,
- receiving breaking alerts,
- returning regularly to see what happens next.

That sense of an ongoing fictional media organization is what transforms the concept from a funny image into a complete digital property.

The final standard is simple:

> **Build the Bovine News Network exactly as seriously as a professional television newsroom would build its own website.**

The cows are the joke.

The website is not.
